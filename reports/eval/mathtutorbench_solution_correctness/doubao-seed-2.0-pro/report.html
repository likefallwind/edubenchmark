Wait—wait, no, let's clarify. The core issue here is the definition of a loan amount. A loan principal (the amount borrowed) is equal to the total amount repaid for a 0% interest loan, which is the sum of the monthly payments: 60 months * $600 = $36,000. A down payment is an upfront out-of-pocket payment that is not part of the loan, as it is not borrowed or repaid as part of the loan.
The problem's parenthetical "including the down payment" is misleading if interpreted literally, because the down payment is not part of the loan. The student incorrectly added the down payment to the total monthly payments to calculate the loan amount, even though the down payment is separate from the loan itself. The 0% interest terms confirm that the loan amount equals the total monthly repayments ($36,000), as that is the full amount borrowed and paid back. The student's addition of the down payment to the loan amount is incorrect, even with the parenthetical, because the down payment is not a component of the loan.
