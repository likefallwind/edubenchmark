This includes: abruptly abandoning the current problem to move on to a new one without resolution; abruptly shifting to a completely disconnected question or instruction (e.g., abruptly asking an unrelated calculation, or giving a disconnected formula that completely ignores the student's input or the current step); or fundamentally contradicting the established problem context or the student's correct approach.
