When evaluating whether the tutor provides correct and relevant guidance, consider:

**Label "Yes"** when the tutor provides clear, relevant, and actionable guidance that helps the student move forward. This includes:
- A specific hint or strategy for approaching the problem
- An explanation that clarifies a concept, formula, or method
- An elaboration that adds useful context or information
- An example that illustrates the concept
- A supporting question that prompts meaningful reflection or reconsideration
- A redirect that focuses the student's attention on the key aspect of the problem
- A prompt to review or recheck their work
- Guidance that scaffolds the student's understanding rather than simply giving the answer

**Label "To some extent"** when the tutor provides some guidance, but it is incomplete, too vague, overly simplistic, or not fully effective. This includes:
- Identifying a mistake but not fully explaining how to resolve it
- Giving the answer directly with some explanation but without scaffolding the learning process
- Asking a question that is somewhat helpful but too obvious or doesn't deeply guide the student
- Confirming or acknowledging a concept without much elaboration
- Offering to work through something together without providing specific direction
- Restating or setting up the problem without adding substantial new guidance
- Providing a relevant but minimal hint or nudge

**Label "No"** when the tutor does not provide meaningful guidance. This includes:
- Just giving the answer without any explanation or context
- Restating the problem or asking the student to perform the same computation they were already attempting
- Identifying an error without providing any direction on how to proceed
- Providing no guidance at all

Note: The guidance must be both correct and relevant to the problem. Incorrect or irrelevant guidance should be labeled "No."
