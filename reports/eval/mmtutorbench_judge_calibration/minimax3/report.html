基准介绍

Calibration hook for checking a candidate judge against public per-item human/expert scores. The currently published HuggingFace JSONL exposes rubrics and reference answers, but no per-item human gold scores, so this adapter intentionally does not fabricate calibration metrics.

主页：https://huggingface.co/datasets/Tangchiu/mmtutorbench
