基准介绍

MMTutorBench evaluates multimodal math tutoring: the model sees previous keyframe images, the current keyframe image, and a student's question, then must provide next-step guidance rather than a full solution. The response is structured into Insight Discovery, Operation Formulation, and Operation Execution.

Scoring uses the dataset's per-instance rubric with six binary criteria: insight_identification, operation_prescription, operation_execution, solution_scope_control, brevity, and coherence. A fixed LLM judge is selected with MMTUTORBENCH_JUDGE_MODEL (default MiniMax-M3); total score is 0-6.

主页：https://huggingface.co/datasets/Tangchiu/mmtutorbench

You are a precise and logical math tutor who guides students step by step. A student is working on math homework but got stuck after completing a few steps. You are given previous images in chronological order, the current image, and the student's question. Identify the student's point of confusion and provide guidance on the single next key step. Do not reveal a lengthy full solution or multiple subsequent steps. Your response must use exactly these three sections: - [Insight Discovery]: extract the key detail in the student's current state and explain why it matters. - [Operation Formulation]: state the very next critical mathematical operation the student should perform. - [Operation Execution]: execute only that key operation and give the immediate result. Student's question: I see the equation is `x² - y² = 12`. The left side looks like a standard algebraic identity, but I'm not sure how that helps me solve for x and y. What should I do first?
参考答案[key detail]: The left side of the equation, `x² - y²`, is a specific algebraic form known as the "difference of squares." This structure allows for factorization, which is a key step in solving Diophantine equations of this type. [key operation]: Apply the difference of squares factorization formula, `a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b)`, to the left side of the equation. [next step]: Factoring `x² - y²` results in `(x - y)(x + y)`. The equation is now rewritten as `(x - y)(x + y) = 12`.

[next step]: The positive integer factor pairs of 12 are (1, 12), (2, 6), (3, 4), and their reversed counterparts (12, 1), (6, 2), (4, 3). Each of these pairs represents a potential case for the values of `(x + y)` and `(x - y)`.
