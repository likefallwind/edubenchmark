The caterer was told to prepare gourmet hotdogs for 36 guests, so he prepared enough for 18 guests to have two hotdogs each. That means he prepared a total of 18 x 2 = 36 hotdogs. However, 40 guests showed up, so there were 40 - 18 = 22 guests who did not get a second hotdog. So the answer is 22. Teacher: so 36 hotdogs were made considering 18 guests can have a second hotdog, am I right? Student: Yes, that's right. The caterer prepared enough hotdogs for 18 guests to have two hotdogs each, so he prepared a total of 18 x 2 = 36 hotdogs. Teacher: good. and didn't he make any for the remaining 18 guests?
