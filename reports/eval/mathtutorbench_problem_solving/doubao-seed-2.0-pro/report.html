正确答案95200
查看模型完整作答
To solve the problem, we break down the given information and make a reasonable, contextually consistent assumption about Lylah's salary increase (since the problem is unsolvable otherwise):
### Step 1: Find Lylah's salary four years ago
We know Adrien earned $40,000 four years ago, and his salary at that time was 30% higher than Lylah's. By definition, "X is 30% higher than Y" means \(X = 1.3Y\) (since 30% higher equals 130% of the base value). Let \(L_4\) = Lylah's salary four years ago:
\[
1.3 \times L_4 = 40000
\]
\[
L_4 = \frac{40000}{1.3} \approx 30769.23
\]
### Step 2: Calculate Adrien's salary four years later
Adrien's salary increased by 40% from his four-year-ago salary:
\[
\text{Adrien's current salary} = 40000 \times 1.4 = 56000
\]
### Step 3: Calculate Lylah's salary four years later
The problem states Adrien's and Lylah's salaries increased *simultaneously*. Since Adrien's 40% increase is explicitly given, we assume Lylah also received the same 40% increase (a standard assumption for salary growth when no other rate is provided, and consistent with the "simultaneous" increase wording):
\[
\text{Lylah's current salary} = L_4 \times 1.4 \approx 30769.23 \times 1.4 \approx 43076.92
\]
### Step 4: Calculate total combined salary
Add Adrien's and Lylah's current salaries:
\[
\text{Total salary} = 56000 + 43076.92 \approx 99076.92
\]
Final answer: 99076.92 (or ~99077 if rounded to the nearest dollar)
---
*Note: If using the common (but mathematically imprecise) interpretation that "30% higher than Lylah's" means Lylah's salary was 30% less than Adrien's four years ago, the total would be $95,200. However, the standard definition of percentage increase yields the result above.*
