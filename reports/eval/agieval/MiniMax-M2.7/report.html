   - *Ginseng* = Ren Shen  
   - *Codonopsis* = Dang Shen  
   - *Atractylodes* in (1) and (4) means **white atractylodes** (Bai Zhu) – the herb that is explicitly named “Atractylodes macrocephala” in (2).  
   - *Atractylodes macrocephala* = Bai Zhu (white atractylodes).  
   - *Shouwu* = He Shou Wu

   1. If Dang Shen is present, Bai Zhu must be present.  
   2. Bai Zhu and Ren Shen cannot both be present (at most one of them).  
   3. Shouwu must be present.  
   4. If Shouwu is present, Bai Zhu must be present.  

   Additionally: **At least one of Ren Shen or Dang Shen must be present.**

   - From (3) and (4): Shouwu is present → Bai Zhu is present.

   - From the mandatory presence of Bai Zhu and condition (2):  
     Bai Zhu and Ren Shen cannot coexist → **Ren Shen must be absent**.

   - Because Ren Shen is absent, the requirement “at least one of Ren Shen or Dang Shen” forces **Dang Shen to be present**.

   - Condition (1) is now automatically satisfied (it also requires Bai Zhu when Dang Shen is present, which we already have).

   - Shouwu (by (3))  
   - Bai Zhu (by (4))  
   - Dang Shen (to satisfy the “ginseng or codonopsis” rule)  
   - No Ren Shen (by the exclusive‑or with Bai Zhu)
