In the diagram, $A B C D$ is a quadrilateral with $A B=B C=C D=6, \angle A B C=90^{\circ}$, and $\angle B C D=60^{\circ}$. Determine the length of $A D$. <image_1>
模型抽取答案\boxed{3(\sqrt6-\sqrt2)}
归一化3(\sqrt6-\sqrt2)
正确答案$6\sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}}$
查看模型完整作答
In \(\triangle BCD\), we have \(BC=CD=6\) and \(\angle BCD=60^\circ\). Thus, by the Law of Cosines,

\[
BD^2=BC^2+CD^2-2(BC)(CD)\cos 60^\circ
=6^2+6^2-2(6)(6)\left(\frac12\right)=36,
\]

so \(BD=6\). Therefore, \(\triangle BCD\) is equilateral, and \(\angle CBD=60^\circ\).

Since \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\), the diagram gives

\[
\angle ABD=90^\circ-60^\circ=30^\circ.
\]

Applying the Law of Cosines in \(\triangle ABD\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
AD^2
&=AB^2+BD^2-2(AB)(BD)\cos30^\circ\\
&=6^2+6^2-2(6)(6)\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\\
&=36(2-\sqrt3).
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence,

\[
AD=6\sqrt{2-\sqrt3}=3(\sqrt6-\sqrt2).
\]

So the final answer is \(\boxed{3(\sqrt6-\sqrt2)}\).

In the diagram, the parabola $$ y=-\frac{1}{4}(x-r)(x-s) $$ intersects the axes at three points. The vertex of this parabola is the point $V$. Determine the value of $k$ and the coordinates of $V$. <image_1>
模型抽取答案\boxed{k=4,\;V=(4,16)}
归一化k=4,\;V=(4,16)
正确答案$4,(4,16)$
查看模型完整作答
The \(x\)-intercepts are the roots of the factorized equation. From the diagram, the roots are \(-k\) and \(3k\), so \(r=-k\) and \(s=3k\). Thus,

\[
y=-\frac14(x+k)(x-3k).
\]

Since the \(y\)-intercept is \((0,3k)\), substitute \(x=0\):

\[
3k=-\frac14(k)(-3k)=\frac{3k^2}{4}.
\]

Hence,

\[
k=\frac{k^2}{4}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
k(k-4)=0.
\]

The value \(k=0\) would make the three intercepts coincide, so \(k=4\).

The vertex lies halfway between the \(x\)-intercepts:

\[
x_V=\frac{-k+3k}{2}=k=4.
\]

\[
y_V=-\frac14(4+4)(4-12)=16.
\]

Therefore, \(V=(4,16)\). So the final answer is \(\boxed{k=4,\;V=(4,16)}\).

In the diagram, eleven circles of four different radius 1, each circle labelled $X$ has radius 2, the circle labelled $Y$ has radius 4 , and the circle labelled $Z$ has radius $r$. Each of the circles labelled $W$ or $X$ is tangent to three other circles. The circle labelled $Y$ is tangent to all ten of the other circles. The circle labelled $Z$ is tangent to three other circles. Determine positive integers $s$ and $t$ for which $r=\frac{s}{t}$. <image_1>
模型抽取答案\boxed{25538,\,2053}
归一化25538,\,2053
正确答案$25538$,$2053$
查看模型完整作答
Let \(O\) be the center of the circle \(Y\), whose radius is \(4\). For two circles of radii \(a\) and \(b\) tangent to each other and to \(Y\), let \(\theta_{a,b}\) be the angle between their centers at \(O\). The three relevant distances are
\[
a+4,\qquad b+4,\qquad a+b.
\]
By the law of cosines and the half-angle identity,
\[
\cos\theta_{a,b}
=\frac{(a+4)^2+(b+4)^2-(a+b)^2}{2(a+4)(b+4)},
\]
and hence
\[
\sin^2\left(\frac{\theta_{a,b}}2\right)
=\frac{ab}{(a+4)(b+4)}.
\]

Let \(\theta\) be the angle between the centers of a \(W\)-circle and an \(X\)-circle. Since their radii are \(1\) and \(2\),
\[
\sin^2\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)
=\frac{1\cdot2}{5\cdot6}
=\frac1{15}.
\]
Therefore,
\[
\cos\theta=1-2\sin^2\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)
=1-\frac2{15}
=\frac{13}{15}.
\]

Around \(Y\), there are eight \(W\)-\(X\) tangencies and two \(X\)-\(Z\) tangencies. Let \(\phi\) be the central angle corresponding to an \(X\)-\(Z\) tangency. Thus,
\[
8\theta+2\phi=2\pi,
\]
so
\[
\frac{\phi}{2}=\frac{\pi}{2}-2\theta.
\]
Consequently,
\[
\sin\left(\frac{\phi}{2}\right)
=\cos(2\theta)
=2\left(\frac{13}{15}\right)^2-1
=\frac{113}{225}.
\]

For the \(X\)-\(Z\) pair, the radii are \(2\) and \(r\), so
\[
\sin^2\left(\frac{\phi}{2}\right)
=\frac{2r}{6(r+4)}
=\frac{r}{3(r+4)}.
\]
Hence
\[
\frac{r}{3(r+4)}
=\left(\frac{113}{225}\right)^2
=\frac{12769}{50625}.
\]
Solving,
\[
50625r=38307(r+4),
\]
\[
12318r=153228,
\]
and therefore
\[
r=\frac{25538}{2053}.
\]
This fraction is in lowest terms, so \(s=25538\) and \(t=2053\).

So the final answer is \(\boxed{25538,\,2053}\).

归一化1250+625\sqrt3
正确答案2500
查看模型完整作答
Place the rectangle on a coordinate plane:
\[
F=(0,0),\quad D=(200,0),\quad A=(0,50),\quad C=(200,50).
\]

Let
\[
B=(x,50),\qquad E=(y,0).
\]
From the congruence \(\triangle FBD\cong\triangle AEC\), the corresponding sides satisfy \(FB=AE\). Hence
\[
x^2+50^2=y^2+50^2,
\]
so \(x=y\). Thus \(B\) and \(E\) are vertically aligned.

Since \(\angle FBD=90^\circ\),
\[
\overrightarrow{BF}\cdot\overrightarrow{BD}=0.
\]
Therefore,
\[
(-x,-50)\cdot(200-x,-50)=0,
\]
which gives
\[
x^2-200x+2500=0.
\]
Thus
\[
x=100\pm50\sqrt3.
\]
As shown in the diagram, \(B\) and \(E\) are nearer \(A\) and \(F\), so
\[
x=100-50\sqrt3.
\]

Let \(P\) be the intersection of \(BD\) and \(EC\). By symmetry, \(P\) lies halfway up the rectangle, so its height is \(25\). Consequently, its horizontal distance from the vertical base \(BE\) is half of \(200-x\):
\[
h=\frac{200-x}{2}
=\frac{200-(100-50\sqrt3)}2
=50+25\sqrt3.
\]

The shaded region is \(\triangle BPE\), with
\[
BE=50.
\]
Hence its area is
\[
\frac12(BE)(h)
=\frac12(50)(50+25\sqrt3)
=1250+625\sqrt3.
\]

So the final answer is \(\boxed{1250+625\sqrt3}\).

正确答案-1,27

Since \(P\) lies on both the \(x\)-axis and the line \(y=3x+3\),

\[
3x_P+3=0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad x_P=-1.
\]

Thus \(P=(-1,0)\). Because \(P\) also lies on the parabola,

\[
0=(-1)^2+t(-1)-2=-1-t,
\]

so

\[
t=-1.
\]

The parabola is therefore

\[
y=x^2-x-2=(x+1)(x-2).
\]

Its other \(x\)-intercept is

\[
Q=(2,0).
\]

To find \(R\), intersect the parabola with \(y=3x+3\):

\[
x^2-x-2=3x+3
\]

\[
x^2-4x-5=0
\]

\[
(x+1)(x-5)=0.
\]

The root \(x=-1\) corresponds to \(P\), so for \(R\),

\[
x_R=5,\qquad y_R=3(5)+3=18.
\]

Hence \(R=(5,18)\). Since \(PQ\) lies on the \(x\)-axis,

\[
PQ=2-(-1)=3.
\]

Therefore, the area of \(\triangle PQR\) is

\[
[\triangle PQR]
=\frac12(3)(18)
=27.
\]

So the final answer is \(\boxed{-1,\ 27}\).
