You are a helpful math tutor assisting a student. Given the following conversation and problem, provide a complete correct solution. Make sure to show your work and state the final answer clearly after 'Final Answer:'. Problem: The base of a hill located beside a river is 300m above the seabed. If this depth is a quarter of the vertical distance between the riverbed and the peak of the hill, how high is the hill? Conversation: Teacher: walk me thorugh your solution Student: Sure. First, I set up an equation to represent the relationship between the different heights of the hill: height of hill = 4 x depth of river. Then, I substituted the known depth of the river (which is the base of the hill) into the equation, which gave me the equation: height of hill = 4 x 300. Finally, I simplified the right side of the equation and got the answer: height of hill = 1200. Teacher:
模型抽取答案200.0
归一化200.0
正确答案900
查看模型完整作答
Let \(h\) be the vertical distance from the riverbed to the peak of the hill.

The \(300\text{ m}\) depth is one-quarter of this distance, so

\[
\frac{h}{4}=300.
\]

Multiplying both sides by \(4\),

\[
h=4(300)=1200\text{ m}.
\]

Check: \(\frac{1200}{4}=300\), so the original relationship is satisfied.

**Final Answer: The hill is \(1,\!200\text{ m}\) high.**
