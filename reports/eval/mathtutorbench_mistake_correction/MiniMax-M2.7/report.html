**Solution**

Let \(x\) be the number of bedbugs the group started with.

Each day the population triples, so after each day we multiply by \(3\).

- After 1 day: \(3x\)
- After 2 days: \(3^2x = 9x\)
- After 3 days: \(3^3x = 27x\)
- After 4 days: \(3^4x = 81x\)

\[

\]

\[

\]

- Day 1: \(3 \times 10 = 30\)
- Day 2: \(3 \times 30 = 90\)

Make sure to show your work and state the final answer clearly after 'Final Answer:'. Problem: Nick's cell phone was initially empty but was then charged for 45 minutes and has reached a 25% charge. 模型抽取答案100.0
归一化100.0
正确答案135

**Step‑by‑step solution**

1. **Identify what is given**  
   - The phone started at 0 % charge.  
   - After charging for 45 minutes it reached 25 % charge.  
   - We assume the charging rate is constant.

2. **Find the charging rate**  
   \[
   \text{Rate} = \frac{25\%}{45\text{ min}} = \frac{25}{45}\% \text{ per minute}
               \approx 0.555\% \text{ per minute.}
   \]

3. **Determine how much charge is still needed**  
   \[
   100\% - 25\% = 75\%.
   \]

4. **Compute the additional time required**  
   Use the rate to find the time \(t\) for 75 %:
   \[
   t = \frac{\text{charge needed}}{\text{rate}} = \frac{75\%}{0.555\%\text{/min}}
     = \frac{75}{25}\times 45\text{ min}
     = 3 \times 45\text{ min}
     = 135\text{ min}.
   \]

   (Equivalently, set up a proportion:  
   \(\displaystyle \frac{45\text{ min}}{25\%} = \frac{t}{75\%}\) → \(t = 45\cdot\frac{75}{25}=135\) min.)

5. **Express the answer in a convenient unit**  
   \(135\) minutes = \(2\) hours \(15\) minutes.

**Why the student's answer was incorrect**

The student wrote “45 minutes × 25 % = 75 %”.  
You cannot multiply minutes by a percentage in this way.  
The correct relationship is a proportion: the ratio *time to charge* is proportional to *percentage increase*.  
Since 45 min gave only 25 % (not 75 %), we must use the proportion shown above.

**Final Answer:** 135 minutes (or 2 hours 15 minutes) of additional charging are needed to reach 100 %.
