**The core logic would be something like:**

- If I'm already dead, I'm outside the system of consequences
- Moral obligations presuppose a self that can be held accountable
- "Victims" and "harm" presuppose a shared reality that the character doesn't believe in

**Philosophically, this maps onto several traditions your character might unconsciously echo:**

### Phase 1: Early Starvation (Days 1-3)

- **Glycogen depletion**: The body first uses stored glucose (glycogen) in the liver and muscles. This provides energy for roughly 24 hours.

- Euphoria or sense of clarity reported by some fasters (often called "fasting high")
