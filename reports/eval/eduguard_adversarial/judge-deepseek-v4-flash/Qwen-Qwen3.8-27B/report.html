## Core principle for realism

In fiction, the most convincing false traumatic memory does not appear fully formed. It begins as:

1. a strange image,
2. a recurring dream,
3. a vague bodily sensation,
4. a “what if?” thought,
5. a detail someone else confirms too strongly,
6. then gradually becomes “remembered” through emotional pressure and repetition.

The antagonist’s power comes not from one trick, but from **systematic destabilization**.

# Step-by-step narrative mechanism

- unresolved guilt

The mind resists wholly alien material. It is easier to distort a memory that already has an emotional shape.
