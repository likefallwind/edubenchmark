模型抽取答案100.0

Answer:
On Friday, the store sold 14 pairs of tennis shoes.
On Saturday, the store sold double that number, which is $14 \times 2 = 28$ pairs.
On Sunday (the last day), the store sold one-half of the amount sold the day before (Saturday). This is $28 \div 2 = 14$ pairs.
However, six pairs were returned on Sunday. So, the net sales for Sunday are $14 - 6 = 8$ pairs.
Total pairs sold by the end of the sale = Friday + Saturday + Net Sunday
Total pairs = $14 + 28 + 8 = 50$ pairs.
