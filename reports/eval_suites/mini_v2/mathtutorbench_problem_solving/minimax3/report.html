Let me work through this step-by-step.

**Step 1:** Find the number of cows per stall after Mr. Sylas distributes the new cows.
- 40 cows divided equally among 20 stalls: 40 ÷ 20 = 2 cows per stall

**Step 2:** Find the total number of cows in each stall.
- Each stall has 20 cows (originally) + 2 new cows = 22 cows per stall

**Step 3:** Find how many cows are in 8 stalls.
- 8 stalls × 22 cows per stall = 176 cows

Final answer: 176
