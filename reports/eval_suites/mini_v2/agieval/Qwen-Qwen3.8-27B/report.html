#gaokao-mathcloze-25task=gaokao-mathcloze language=zh question_type=cloze
问题：已知 $a, b, c$ 分别为 $\triangle A B C$ 的三个内角 $A, B, C$ 的对边, $a=2$ 且 $(2+b)(\sin A-\sin B)=(c-b) \sin C$, 则 $\triangle A B C$ 面积的最大值为 $(\quad)$. 请逐步推导，并在最后用 \boxed{} 给出最终答案。
模型抽取答案\sqrt3
归一化\sqrt3
正确答案$\sqrt{3}$
查看模型完整作答
由正弦定理，设

\[
\frac{\sin A}{a}=\frac{\sin B}{b}=\frac{\sin C}{c}=k,
\]

则

\[
\sin A=ak,\quad \sin B=bk,\quad \sin C=ck.
\]

已知 \(a=2\)，代入题设条件：

\[
(2+b)(\sin A-\sin B)=(c-b)\sin C
\]

得

\[
(2+b)(2k-bk)=(c-b)ck.
\]

因为 \(k>0\)，约去 \(k\)：

\[
(2+b)(2-b)=c(c-b).
\]

即

\[
4-b^2=c^2-bc.
\]

整理得

\[
b^2+c^2-bc=4. \tag{1}
\]

另一方面，由余弦定理：

\[
a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A.
\]

因为 \(a=2\)，所以

\[
4=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A. \tag{2}
\]

比较 \((1)\) 与 \((2)\)：

\[
b^2+c^2-bc=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos A,
\]

因此

\[
bc=2bc\cos A.
\]

由于 \(b,c>0\)，所以

\[
\cos A=\frac12,
\]

故

\[
A=\frac{\pi}{3}.
\]

三角形面积为

\[
S=\frac12 bc\sin A
=\frac12 bc\cdot \frac{\sqrt3}{2}
=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}bc.
\]

下面在约束条件

\[
b^2+c^2-bc=4
\]

下求 \(bc\) 的最大值。由均值不等式：

\[
b^2+c^2\ge 2bc,
\]

所以

\[
4=b^2+c^2-bc\ge 2bc-bc=bc.
\]

因此

\[
bc\le 4.
\]

等号成立当且仅当 \(b=c\)。此时由

\[
b^2+c^2-bc=4
\]

得

\[
b^2=4,
\]

即

\[
b=c=2.
\]

此时三角形为边长为 \(2\) 的等边三角形，面积取得最大值：

\[
S_{\max}=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\cdot 4=\sqrt3.
\]

因此，\(\triangle ABC\) 面积的最大值为

\[
\boxed{\sqrt3}
\]

Based on the following questions? Most people think that advertisers do whatever they can to attract customers.But advertisers are not always like this.Recently, in order to expand sales, a family magazine called "Bay" was renamed "Inferno", which mainly published violent and pornographic content.As a result, some perennial advertisers from the original "Bay" magazine refused to renew the contract and turned to other publications.This shows that these advertisers not only consider economic benefits, but also take into account their ethical responsibilities. Question: If the following item is true, can the argument be strengthened most? Answer Choices: (A)The cost and selling price of "Inferno" are lower than that of "Harbour". (B)The above advertisers who refused to renew their contracts have not affected the benefits after switching to other publications. (C)Readers of family magazines are generally not interested in violent and pornographic content. (D)Some clients who advertise in other family magazines and so on turn to "Inferno" magazine. Think step by step, then end with a line "The answer is X" (give all correct option letters if multiple).
模型抽取答案B
归一化B
正确答案D
查看模型完整作答
The argument concludes that the advertisers’ refusal to renew with *Inferno* shows they consider ethical responsibilities, not only economic benefits.

To strengthen this, we need to make it more plausible that their decision was not simply driven by economic self-interest or economic loss.

- **(A)** says *Inferno* has lower cost and selling price than the original magazine. This may make the magazine seem more economically attractive, but it does not directly show that these advertisers’ economic benefits were unaffected by their decision.
- **(B)** directly says that after switching to other publications, these advertisers’ benefits were not affected. This weakens the alternative explanation that they left only because *Inferno* would harm their economic interests, making the ethical-responsibility explanation more plausible.
- **(C)** suggests readers may dislike violent/pornographic content, which gives an economic reason for advertisers to leave; this weakens the argument.
- **(D)** mentions other clients turning to *Inferno*, but it does not directly address these advertisers’ motives or benefits.

Thus, (B) most strongly strengthens the argument.

The answer is B

We use the product-to-sum identity:

\[
\cos A\cos B=\frac12\left(\cos(A-B)+\cos(A+B)\right)
\]

First,

\[
\cos 54^\circ \cos 4^\circ
= \frac12\left(\cos 50^\circ+\cos 58^\circ\right)
\]

Next,

\[
\cos 36^\circ \cos 86^\circ
= \frac12\left(\cos(-50^\circ)+\cos 122^\circ\right)
= \frac12\left(\cos 50^\circ+\cos 122^\circ\right)
\]

Subtract:

\[
\cos 54^\circ \cos 4^\circ-\cos 36^\circ \cos 86^\circ
= \frac12\left(\cos 50^\circ+\cos 58^\circ-\cos 50^\circ-\cos 122^\circ\right)
\]

\[
= \frac12\left(\cos 58^\circ-\cos 122^\circ\right)
\]

Now use

\[
\cos x-\cos y=-2\sin\left(\frac{x+y}{2}\right)\sin\left(\frac{x-y}{2}\right)
\]

So,

\[
\cos 58^\circ-\cos 122^\circ
= -2\sin 90^\circ \sin(-32^\circ)
\]

Since \(\sin 90^\circ=1\) and \(\sin(-32^\circ)=-\sin 32^\circ\),

\[
\cos 58^\circ-\cos 122^\circ
= 2\sin 32^\circ
\]

Thus,

\[
\frac12\left(\cos 58^\circ-\cos 122^\circ\right)=\sin 32^\circ
\]

\[
\boxed{\text{sin 32}}
