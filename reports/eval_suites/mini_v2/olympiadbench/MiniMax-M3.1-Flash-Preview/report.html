#OE_MM_maths_en_COMP-2491subject=Math language=en modality=MM answer_type=Tuple,Numerical
The following is an open-ended problem from an International Math competition. Please calculate the answer according to the given requirements and the information provided. Please use LaTeX format to represent the variables and formulas used in the solution process and results. Please end your solution with "So the final answer is \boxed{multiple answers connected with commas}." and give the result explicitly. In the diagram, the circle with centre $C(1,1)$ passes through the point $O(0,0)$, intersects the $y$-axis at $A$, and intersects the $x$-axis at $B(2,0)$. Determine, with justification, the coordinates of $A$ and the area of the part of the circle that lies in the first quadrant. <image_1>
模型抽取答案\boxed{A=(0,2),\ \text{Area}=2+\pi}
归一化A=(0,2),\ \text{Area}=2+\pi
正确答案$(0,2),\pi+2$
查看模型完整作答
The radius is determined by the distance from \(C\) to \(O\):

\[
r=CO=\sqrt{(1-0)^2+(1-0)^2}=\sqrt2.
\]

Hence the equation of the circle is

\[
(x-1)^2+(y-1)^2=2.
\]

On the \(y\)-axis, \(x=0\), so

\[
1+(y-1)^2=2
\]

and therefore

\[
(y-1)^2=1 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=0\ \text{or}\ y=2.
\]

The first point is \(O\), so the other intersection is

\[
A=(0,2).
\]

Now

\[
\frac{A+B}{2}
=\left(\frac{0+2}{2},\frac{2+0}{2}\right)
=(1,1)=C,
\]

so \(AB\) is a diameter of the circle. The part of the circle in the first quadrant consists of:

- the right triangle \(AOB\), whose area is
  \[
  \frac12(2)(2)=2;
  \]
- the semicircle with diameter \(AB\), whose area is
  \[
  \frac12\pi r^2=\frac12\pi(\sqrt2)^2=\pi.
  \]

Thus the required area is

\[
2+\pi.
\]

So the final answer is \(\boxed{A=(0,2),\ \text{Area}=2+\pi}\).
