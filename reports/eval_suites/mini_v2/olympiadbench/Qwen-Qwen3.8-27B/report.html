## Setting up the conservation laws

From 3.3 the interaction potential is

$$U(r)=-\frac{\alpha Q^{2}}{32\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}}\frac{1}{r^{4}}$$

Because $M\gg m$ the atom is a fixed centre, so both energy and angular momentum of the ion are conserved.

**Energy** (initially $U\to0$, speed $v_0$):
$$\tfrac{1}{2}m v_0^{2}=\tfrac{1}{2}m v^{2}-\frac{\alpha Q^{2}}{32\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}}\frac{1}{r_{\min}^{4}}$$

**Angular momentum** (at closest approach the velocity is purely transverse, so $L=m v r_{\min}=m v_0 b$):
$$v=v_0\,\frac{b}{r_{\min}}$$

## Solving for $r_{\min}$

Substituting $v$ and writing $z=r_{\min}^{2}$:

$$\tfrac{1}{2}m v_0^{2}=\tfrac{1}{2}m v_0^{2}\frac{b^{2}}{z}-\frac{\alpha Q^{2}}{32\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}}\frac{1}{z^{2}}$$

Multiplying by $2z^{2}$ and rearranging gives the quadratic

$$m v_0^{2}\,z^{2}-m v_0^{2}b^{2}\,z+\frac{\alpha Q^{2}}{16\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}}=0$$

$$z=r_{\min}^{2}=\frac{b^{2}\pm\sqrt{b^{4}-\dfrac{\alpha Q^{2}}{4\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}m v_0^{2}}}}{2}$$

The physical (scattering) turning point is the **larger** root (the outer allowed region $r\in(r_{\min},\infty)$; the smaller root lies in the disconnected inner region). A quick check: for $\alpha\to0$ this yields $r_{\min}=b$, as required for a straight line, and for finite $\alpha$ one gets $r_{\min}<b$, consistent with the attraction.

Hence

$$r_{\min}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left(b^{2}+\sqrt{b^{4}-\frac{\alpha Q^{2}}{4\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}m v_0^{2}}}\right)$$

(real solutions require $b^{4}\ge \alpha Q^{2}/(4\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}m v_0^{2})$; below this critical $b$ the ion is captured by the atom).

So the final answer is \boxed{r_{\min}=\sqrt{\dfrac{b^{2}+\sqrt{b^{4}-\dfrac{\alpha Q^{2}}{4\pi^{2}\varepsilon_{0}^{2}m v_{0}^{2}}}}{2}}}
