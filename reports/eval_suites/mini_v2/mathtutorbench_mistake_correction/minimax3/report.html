Looking at this problem, I need to find the length of the board **before** Tom cut the 7 cm off.

## Setting Up the Problem

Let me trace through what happened to Tom's board:
- **Original length:** 143 cm
- **After cutting 25 cm off:** 143 − 25 = 118 cm
- **After cutting 7 cm off:** 118 − 7 = 111 cm

## Identifying the Question

The question asks: "How long were the boards **before** he cut the 7 cm off?"

The 7 cm cut was made *after* the 25 cm cut. So the length **before** the 7 cm cut is the length **after** the 25 cm cut.

$$\text{Length before 7 cm cut} = 143 - 25 = 118 \text{ cm}$$

## Checking the Student's Work

The student made an error by **adding** 7 cm to 118 cm. However, the 7 cm was cut *off* the board, not added to it. The 118 cm length already represents the board's size right before that final cut occurred.

- Student's calculation: 118 + 7 = 125 cm ❌ (incorrectly added 7 cm)
- Correct calculation: 118 cm ✓ (this IS the length before the 7 cm cut)

**Final Answer: 118 cm**

**Hi! Let me walk through your solution to find the error.**

You correctly identified:
- Emma's time per arena: 10 hours
- Fernando's time per arena: 20 hours

**But here's the issue:** When two people work **together** on the same task, you need to add their **rates** (how much they can do per hour), not their times. Simply adding times only works if they are taking turns, not running side by side.

Let's fix it step by step:

**Step 1: Find each person's rate (arenas per hour)**

- Emma: 2 arenas in 20 hours → rate = 2/20 = **1/10 arena per hour**
- Fernando: 2 arenas in 40 hours (twice Emma's time) → rate = 2/40 = **1/20 arena per hour**

**Step 2: Add the rates to get their combined rate**

- Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/20
- Find a common denominator: = 2/20 + 1/20 = **3/20 arenas per hour**

**Step 3: Find the time to complete 2 arenas together**

- Time = Work ÷ Rate
- Time = 2 ÷ (3/20) = 2 × (20/3) = **40/3 hours**

**Step 4: Convert to a mixed number (optional)**

- 40/3 = 13⅓ hours, or about **13 hours and 20 minutes**

**Why your method was off:**
You added per-arena times (10 + 20 = 30 hours), but this assumes they take turns running. Since the question asks how long it takes them to run **together**, you must add their rates — the more one person helps, the faster the job gets done.

**A good rule of thumb:**
- If they work **together at the same time** → add the **rates**
- If they work **one after the other** → add the **times**

Final Answer: 40/3 hours (or about 13 hours and 20 minutes)

$$\text{Lunch time} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Reading time}$$
