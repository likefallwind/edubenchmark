Fernando takes twice as long as Emma to run his course, so it takes him 40 hours to run around two arenas. This means it takes him 20 hours to run around one arena. 模型抽取答案20.0
归一化20.0
正确答案60
查看模型完整作答
The student's approach mistakenly added the times instead of combining rates. When two people work together, their rates add, not their times.

**Correct Solution:**

1. **Find Emma's rate:**  
   Emma runs 2 arenas in 20 hours.  
   Rate = Distance ÷ Time = \( \frac{2 \text{ arenas}}{20 \text{ hours}} = \frac{1}{10} \) arena per hour.

2. **Find Fernando's rate:**  
   Fernando takes twice as long as Emma for the same course (2 arenas), so his time is \( 2 \times 20 = 40 \) hours.  
   Rate = \( \frac{2 \text{ arenas}}{40 \text{ hours}} = \frac{1}{20} \) arena per hour.

3. **Combine their rates:**  
   Combined rate = \( \frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{20} = \frac{2}{20} + \frac{1}{20} = \frac{3}{20} \) arenas per hour.

4. **Find the time to run 2 arenas together:**  
   Time = Distance ÷ Combined rate = \( 2 \div \frac{3}{20} = 2 \times \frac{20}{3} = \frac{40}{3} \) hours.  
   \( \frac{40}{3} \) hours = 13\(\frac{1}{3}\) hours = **13 hours and 20 minutes**.

**Final Answer:** \(\frac{40}{3}\) hours, or 13 hours and 20 minutes.
