正确答案125

Good start, but let’s keep the cuts in order.

- Tom first cut the wood to **143 cm**.
- He then cut **25 cm** off:  
  \[
  143 - 25 = 118
  \]
  So after that cut, the board was **118 cm** long.

The **7 cm** cut happened **after** the board was already 118 cm long. So, before he cut off the 7 cm, the board was still **118 cm** long.

You do not add 7 here. Adding 7 would be correct if 118 cm were the length **after** the 7 cm cut. If we wanted the length **after** the 7 cm cut, it would be:
\[
118 - 7 = 111
\]

Final Answer: 118 cm

   Jerry needs 500 cherries per quart.

   \[

   \]

Hi Winnie, your percent calculations are correct, but let’s separate two things:

- **Selling price** of the house
- **Amount left after paying a commission**, if a commission was paid

**Step 1: Find the profit.**  
Mrs. Choi bought the house for \$80,000.  
A 20% profit on the original price is:

\[
0.20 \times 80000 = 16000
\]

**Step 2: Find the selling price before commission.**  
Add the profit to the original price:

\[
80000 + 16000 = 96000
\]

So the house **sold for** \$96,000.

**Step 3: Consider the broker’s commission.**  
A 5% commission from the original price is:

\[
0.05 \times 80000 = 4000
\]

\[
96000 - 4000 = 92000
\]

But the question asks **how much the house sold for**, which is the selling price, not the amount left after commission.

**Final Answer: \$96,000**
