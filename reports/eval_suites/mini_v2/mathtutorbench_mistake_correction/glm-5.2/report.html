You started off really well by subtracting 25 cm from 143 cm to get 118 cm. That is the correct length of the board after the first cut! 

However, you got a bit confused with the order of the steps. Let's break down the sequence of events:
1. Tom first cuts the wood to 143 cm.
2. He then cuts 25 cm off, making the board 143 cm - 25 cm = 118 cm.
3. He realizes this board is still too long compared to the other boards, so he plans to cut another 7 cm off.

The question asks: "How long were the boards *before* he cut the 7 cm off?"

Since the 7 cm cut happens *after* the 25 cm cut, the length of the boards before the 7 cm cut is simply the length after the 25 cm cut. 

You don't need to add or subtract the 7 cm at all to find the length *before* that specific cut! (If the question had asked for the length *after* he cut the 7 cm off, you would subtract 7 from 118 to get 111 cm).

* Length of the board after Tom cuts off 25 cm: 143 cm - 25 cm = 118 cm
* Length before the 7 cm cut: 118 cm

Teacher: You correctly determined that Fernando takes 40 hours to run around the two arenas! However, when two people work together on a task, we cannot simply add their individual times for a smaller part of the task. Instead, we need to add their work rates to find their combined speed. 

Here is the step-by-step correct solution:

1. **Identify their individual rates:**
- Emma can run 2 arenas in 20 hours. Her rate is 2 arenas / 20 hours = **1/10 of an arena per hour**.
- Fernando takes twice as long as Emma to run the same course (2 arenas). So, he takes 2 × 20 = 40 hours. His rate is 2 arenas / 40 hours = **1/20 of an arena per hour**.

2. **Calculate their combined rate:**
- When Emma and Fernando run together, their rates add up.
- Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/20 
- To add these, find a common denominator: 2/20 + 1/20 = **3/20 of an arena per hour**.

3. **Find the total time to run around 2 arenas together:**
- Time = Total Distance ÷ Combined Rate
- Time = 2 arenas ÷ (3/20 arenas per hour)
- Time = 2 × (20/3) = **40/3 hours**.

40/3 hours is the same as 13 1/3 hours, or 13 hours and 20 minutes.

Final Answer: 13 1/3 hours (or 13 hours and 20 minutes)
