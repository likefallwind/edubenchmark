I started by letting x be the number of bedbugs the group started with. Then, I used the given information to calculate the number of bedbugs after each day. 正确答案30
查看模型完整作答
Your reasoning is correct. Let \(x\) be the initial number of bedbugs. Since the number triples each day:

- After 1 day: \(3x\)
- After 2 days: \(3^2x = 9x\)
- After 3 days: \(3^3x = 27x\)
- After 4 days: \(3^4x = 81x\)

There were 810 bedbugs after four days, so:
\[
81x = 810
\]
\[
x = \frac{810}{81} = 10
\]

Checking: \(10 \to 30 \to 90 \to 270 \to 810\).

Final Answer: The group started with **10 bedbugs**.

   \[
   110 \times 120 = 13{,}200\text{ square feet}
   \]

2. **Find the total distance Bob must travel with the tiller.**  
   Since the tiller covers a swath 2 feet wide:
   \[
   \frac{13{,}200\text{ square feet}}{2\text{ feet}}=6{,}600\text{ feet}
   \]

   Each foot takes 2 seconds:
   \[
   6{,}600\times2=13{,}200\text{ seconds}
   \]

   \[
   \frac{13{,}200}{60}=220\text{ minutes}
   \]
