Looking at this problem, I need to find the length of the board **before** Tom cut the 7 cm off.

## Setting Up the Problem

Let me trace through what happened to Tom's board:
- **Original length:** 143 cm
- **After cutting 25 cm off:** 143 − 25 = 118 cm
- **After cutting 7 cm off:** 118 − 7 = 111 cm

## Identifying the Question

The question asks: "How long were the boards **before** he cut the 7 cm off?"

The 7 cm cut was made *after* the 25 cm cut. So the length **before** the 7 cm cut is the length **after** the 25 cm cut.

$$\text{Length before 7 cm cut} = 143 - 25 = 118 \text{ cm}$$

## Checking the Student's Work

The student made an error by **adding** 7 cm to 118 cm. However, the 7 cm was cut *off* the board, not added to it. The 118 cm length already represents the board's size right before that final cut occurred.

- Student's calculation: 118 + 7 = 125 cm ❌ (incorrectly added 7 cm)
- Correct calculation: 118 cm ✓ (this IS the length before the 7 cm cut)

**Final Answer: 118 cm**

$$35 - 23 = 12 \text{ years}$$

So John is 12 years older than James.

**Step 2: Set up variables.**

Let James's current age = $x$

Then John's current age = $x + 12$

**Step 3: Translate "Tim is 5 years less than twice John's age" into an equation.**

$$\text{Tim} = 2(\text{John}) - 5$$

$$2(x + 12) - 5 = 79$$

$$2x + 24 - 5 = 79$$

$$2x = 60$$

- John = 30 + 12 = 42
