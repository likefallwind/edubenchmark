\[

\]

Ashley initially invited 20 friends. Each of those friends invited one additional person, so the number of invited guests was

\[
20+20=40.
\]

Half of these invited guests came with one additional person:

\[
\frac{40}{2}=20.
\]

Thus, the 40 invited guests were joined by 20 more people:

\[
40+20=60 \text{ guests}.
\]

Finally, include Ashley:

\[
60+1=61.
\]

The earlier solution of \(41\) omitted the 20 invited guests who attended without bringing an additional person.

**Final Answer: 61 people.**
