Great work, Jia! Your reasoning is correct. Let me walk through the solution step by step to confirm everything.

**Setting Up the Problem**

Let $x$ be the number of bedbugs the group started with.

Since the number of bedbugs **triples** each day, we multiply by 3 for every day that passes:

- **Start:** $x$ bedbugs
- **After Day 1:** $3x$ bedbugs
- **After Day 2:** $3 \cdot 3x = 9x$ bedbugs
- **After Day 3:** $3 \cdot 9x = 27x$ bedbugs
- **After Day 4:** $3 \cdot 27x = 81x$ bedbugs

**Writing the Equation**

We know that after 4 days, there were 810 bedbugs, so:

$$81x = 810$$

**Solving for x**

$$x = \frac{810}{81} = 10$$

**Checking the Answer**

Let's verify by counting day by day starting from 10 bedbugs:
- Day 1: $10 \times 3 = 30$
- Day 2: $30 \times 3 = 90$
- Day 3: $90 \times 3 = 270$
- Day 4: $270 \times 3 = 810$ ✓

This matches the given information, so your answer of $x = 10$ is exactly right. Nicely done — your equation $81x = 810$ was set up correctly, and your solution is complete!

Final Answer: The group started with **10 bedbugs**.
