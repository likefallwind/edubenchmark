We are given:
- James turned 23 at the same time John turned 35.  
- Tim is 5 years less than twice John's age.  
- Tim is currently 79.

We need to find James's current age.

**Step 1: Find the age difference between James and John.**  
When James was 23, John was 35. The difference in their ages is constant:  
35 – 23 = 12 years.

Let James's current age be \( J \). Then John's current age is \( J + 12 \).

79 = 2(\( J + 12 \)) – 5

79 = 2J + 24 – 5  
79 = 2J + 19

60 = 2J

The student's interpretation assumes that only half of the invited guests attended. However, the problem states that *half of the invited guests each came with one more person* — it does not say the other half failed to attend. The most reasonable reading is that **all** invited guests came to the party, but half of them brought an extra (uninvited) person.

Let's solve it step by step.

1. **Total invited guests**  
   Ashley invited 20 friends. She told each of those 20 friends to invite one more person.  
   So the friends' invitees = 20.  
   Total invited guests = 20 (Ashley's friends) + 20 (their invitees) = **40 people**.

2. **People who brought an extra person**  
   The problem says: "half the number of the invited guests each came with one more person."  
   Half of 40 invited guests = 40 ÷ 2 = **20 people**.  
   These 20 invited guests each brought **one extra person**, so they added another 20 people who were not originally on the invitation list.

3. **Total attendees**  
   * All 40 invited guests attended the party.  
   * 20 of them brought an extra person, adding 20 more people.  
   * Ashley herself is also at the party.  

   Total = 40 (all invited guests) + 20 (extra people) + 1 (Ashley) = **61 people**.

Final Answer: **61**
