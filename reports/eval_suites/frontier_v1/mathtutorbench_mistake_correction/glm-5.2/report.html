You started off really well by subtracting 25 cm from 143 cm to get 118 cm. That is the correct length of the board after the first cut! 

However, you got a bit confused with the order of the steps. Let's break down the sequence of events:
1. Tom first cuts the wood to 143 cm.
2. He then cuts 25 cm off, making the board 143 cm - 25 cm = 118 cm.
3. He realizes this board is still too long compared to the other boards, so he plans to cut another 7 cm off.

The question asks: "How long were the boards *before* he cut the 7 cm off?"

Since the 7 cm cut happens *after* the 25 cm cut, the length of the boards before the 7 cm cut is simply the length after the 25 cm cut. 

You don't need to add or subtract the 7 cm at all to find the length *before* that specific cut! (If the question had asked for the length *after* he cut the 7 cm off, you would subtract 7 from 118 to get 111 cm).

Here is the correct step-by-step work:
* Length of the board after Tom cuts off 25 cm: 143 cm - 25 cm = 118 cm
* Length before the 7 cm cut: 118 cm

Final Answer: 118 cm
