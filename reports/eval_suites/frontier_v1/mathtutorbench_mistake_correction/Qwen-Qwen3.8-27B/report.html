Student: Sure. I started by figuring out the length of the board after Tom cut off 25 cm. I did this by subtracting 25 from 143, which gave me 118 cm. 正确答案125

Good start, but let’s keep the cuts in order.

- Tom first cut the wood to **143 cm**.
- He then cut **25 cm** off:  
  \[
  143 - 25 = 118
  \]
  So after that cut, the board was **118 cm** long.

The **7 cm** cut happened **after** the board was already 118 cm long. So, before he cut off the 7 cm, the board was still **118 cm** long.

You do not add 7 here. Adding 7 would be correct if 118 cm were the length **after** the 7 cm cut. If we wanted the length **after** the 7 cm cut, it would be:
\[
118 - 7 = 111
\]

Final Answer: 118 cm

Michael, let’s break the problem down step by step and make sure we count each child’s hotdogs exactly once.

1. **Ella and Emma’s hotdogs**  
   Ella wants 2 hotdogs.  
   Emma wants 2 hotdogs.  
   So the sisters together want:  
   \[
   2 + 2 = 4
   \]

2. **Luke’s hotdogs**  
   Luke wants **twice the amount as his sisters**. Since his sisters together want 4 hotdogs, Luke wants:  
   \[
   2 \times 4 = 8
   \]

3. **Hunter’s hotdogs**  
   Hunter wants **1 and a half times the total amount of his sisters**. The sisters’ total is 4, so Hunter wants:  
   \[
   1.5 \times 4 = 6
   \]

4. **Total hotdogs Matthew needs to cook**  
   Add each child’s amount once:  
   \[
   2 + 2 + 8 + 6 = 18
   \]

   Or, using the sisters’ combined total:  
   \[
   4 + 8 + 6 = 18
   \]

So Matthew needed to cook **18 hotdogs**.

Final Answer: 18 hotdogs

- **Each person gets the same number of brownies as everyone else**

- The coach starts with 8 liters of water.
- Convert liters to milliliters:  
  \(8 \text{ L} = 8000 \text{ mL}\)

- Water needed for all players:  
  \(30 \times 200 = 6000 \text{ mL}\)

- Water spilled:
