Answer Choices: (A)Franz's oil, Franz's watercolor, Greene's oil, Isaacs's oil (B)Franz's oil, Hidalgo's watercolor, Isaacs's watercolor, Isaacs's oil (C)Greene's oil, Franz's oil, Isaacs's oil, Hidalgo's oil (D)Hidalgo's oil, Greene's oil, Greene's watercolor, Isaacs's oil (E)Hidalgo's watercolor, Franz's oil, Greene's oil, Isaacs's oil Think step by step, then end with a line "The answer is X" (give all correct option letters if multiple).
模型抽取答案A
归一化A
正确答案C
查看模型完整作答
Let \(FO\) mean Franz’s oil, \(GW\) Greene’s watercolor, and so on.

Because \(GW\) is in the upper position on the wall containing \(FO\), that wall must have **\(GW\) above \(FO\)**. Also, \(IO\) must be lower on wall 4.

- **(C)** is impossible because wall 4’s lower painting is \(HO\), not \(IO\).
- **(D)** is impossible because it places \(GW\) in a lower position.
- **(E)** leaves \(FW\), \(HO\), and \(IW\) as the possible upper paintings for walls 1, 3, and 4. On wall 1, above \(HW\), either watercolor would create an all-watercolor wall, while \(HO\) would put two Hidalgo paintings on the same wall. Thus **(E)** is impossible.
- In **(B)**, the upper paintings left after placing \(GW\) are \(FW\), \(GO\), and \(HO\). Wall 2 must use \(GO\) above \(HW\): \(FW\) would make two watercolors, and \(HO\) would make both paintings Hidalgo’s. Wall 3 must then use \(HO\) above \(IW\), since Franz cannot share a wall with Isaacs. This forces \(FW\) above \(IO\) on wall 4, illegally placing Franz and Isaacs together.

**(A)** works with the following arrangement:

| Wall | Upper | Lower |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greene’s watercolor | Franz’s oil |
| 2 | Hidalgo’s oil | Franz’s watercolor |
| 3 | Isaacs’s watercolor | Greene’s oil |
| 4 | Hidalgo’s watercolor | Isaacs’s oil |

Every wall has an oil, contains work by two students, and avoids placing Franz with Isaacs.

The answer is A

Question: The number of water lilies on a certain lake doubles every two days. If there is exactly one water lily on the lake, it takes 60 days for the lake to be fully covered with water lilies. In how many days will the lake be fully covered with lilies, if initially there were 64 water lilies on it? Answer Choices: (A)15 (B)28 (C)30 (D)53 (E)59 Think step by step, then end with a line "The answer is X" (give all correct option letters if multiple).
模型抽取答案E
归一化E
正确答案D
查看模型完整作答
Starting with one lily, the population doubles \(60/2=30\) times before the lake is full, so full coverage corresponds to \(2^{30}\) lilies.

Starting with \(64=2^6\) lilies, only \(30-6=24\) more doublings are needed. At two days per doubling, this takes
\[
24 \times 2 = 48\text{ days}.
\]

Thus, the question or answer choices contain an error, since 48 days is not listed.

The answer is 48 days (none of the listed choices).
