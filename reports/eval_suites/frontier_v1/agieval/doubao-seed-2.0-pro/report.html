To solve the problem, we analyze the exponential growth of the water lilies step by step:

### Step 1: Understand the growth pattern
The water lilies **double every 2 days**, meaning their doubling period is 2 days. For an initial count of \(N_0\) lilies, the number of lilies after \(t\) days is given by:
\[ N(t) = N_0 \times 2^{(t/2)} \]
This works because every 2 days, the exponent increases by 1 (e.g., at \(t=2\), \(N=2N_0\); at \(t=4\), \(N=4N_0\), etc.).

### Step 2: Calculate the number of lilies needed for full coverage
When starting with 1 lily (\(N_0=1\)), the lake is fully covered at \(t=60\) days. Let \(F\) be the number of lilies required for full coverage:
\[ F = N(60) = 1 \times 2^{(60/2)} = 2^{30} \]

### Step 3: Model growth for the initial 64 lilies
Now, we start with 64 lilies. Note that \(64 = 2^6\). We need to find the time \(t\) when the number of lilies equals \(F=2^{30}\):
\[ N(t) = 64 \times 2^{(t/2)} = 2^6 \times 2^{(t/2)} = 2^{(6 + t/2)} \]

### Step 4: Solve for \(t\)
Set the expression equal to \(F=2^{30}\). Since the bases are equal, their exponents must be equal:
\[ 6 + \frac{t}{2} = 30 \]
Subtract 6 from both sides:
\[ \frac{t}{2} = 24 \]
Multiply by 2:
\[ t = 48 \]

### Note on the answer choices
The mathematically correct answer for the problem as stated is 48 days, which is not listed in the provided options. This suggests a potential typo in the problem or answer choices.

The answer is 48 (not listed in the given options).

问题：（5 分) 不等式 $\sqrt{2 x^{2}+1}-x \leqslant 1$ 的解集是 $(\quad)$. 请逐步推导，并在最后用 \boxed{} 给出最终答案。
模型抽取答案[0,2]
归一化[0,2]
正确答案$[0,2]$
查看模型完整作答
### 步骤1：对原不等式进行等价变形
原不等式为 $\sqrt{2 x^{2}+1}-x \leqslant 1$，将 $-x$ 移到右侧，得到：
$$\sqrt{2x^2+1} \leqslant x + 1$$
由于左侧根号的结果非负（$\sqrt{2x^2+1} \geqslant 1 > 0$），因此右侧必须满足非负条件，否则不等式不可能成立，即：
$$x + 1 \geqslant 0 \implies x \geqslant -1$$
---
### 步骤2：两边平方去根号
在 $x \geqslant -1$ 的前提下，不等式两边均为非负数，平方后不等号方向不变，因此对两边同时平方：
$$\left(\sqrt{2x^2+1}\right)^2 \leqslant (x+1)^2$$
展开后得到：
$$2x^2 + 1 \leqslant x^2 + 2x + 1$$
---
### 步骤3：求解二次不等式
将右侧项移到左侧并整理：
$$2x^2 + 1 - x^2 - 2x - 1 \leqslant 0$$
化简得：
$$x^2 - 2x \leqslant 0$$
因式分解为：
$$x(x - 2) \leqslant 0$$
二次函数 $y=x(x-2)$ 开口向上，因此不等式的解为两根之间，即：
$$0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$$
---
### 步骤4：结合前提条件取交集
之前得到前提 $x \geqslant -1$，而 $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$ 完全包含在 $x \geqslant -1$ 范围内，因此最终解集无需额外剔除。
---
### 验证（可选）
- 取端点 $x=0$：左侧 $\sqrt{0+1}-0=1 \leqslant 1$，成立；
- 取端点 $x=2$：左侧 $\sqrt{8+1}-2=3-2=1 \leqslant 1$，成立；
- 取区间内点 $x=1$：左侧 $\sqrt{2+1}-1=\sqrt{3}-1 \approx 0.732 \leqslant 1$，成立；
- 取区间外点 $x=-1$：左侧 $\sqrt{2+1} - (-1)=\sqrt{3}+1 \approx 2.732 >1$，不成立，符合推导结果。
最终解集为 $\boxed{[0,2]}$（或写成 $\{x \mid 0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2\}$）。

### 选项D分析
