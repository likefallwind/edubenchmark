For young people 13–17, solve clues matching each friend with her item and spot on large four-cushion seating piece.

## Scenario
Four friends (Ami, Blu, Jai, Rio) each placed one unique item on seating piece in shared living space. Items placed: cozy wool scarf, vintage vinyl disc, new paperback book, hand-shaped ceramic jar. Each friend claimed one spot on seating piece for placing her item. Spots are ordered 1 through 4, with 1 nearest entryway and 4 farthest from entryway.

## Clues
1. Person who placed jar never put item in an end spot.
2. Ami placed her item with precisely one empty spot between hers and vinyl disc’s owner’s item.
3. Blu’s spot comes before Jai’s spot in position order.
4. Scarf rests in spot farthest from entryway.
5. Rio’s item occupies a lower-numbered spot, followed by paperback book, followed by jar.

## Questions
1. Which item is Rio’s?
   A) Cozy wool scarf
   B) Vintage vinyl disc
   C) New paperback book
   D) Hand-shaped ceramic jar

2. Which friend placed her item in spot 2?
   A) Ami
   B) Blu
   C) Jai
   D) Rio

3. Which item occupies spot 1, nearest entryway?
   A) Vintage vinyl disc
   B) New paperback book
   C) Hand-shaped ceramic jar
   D) Cozy wool scarf

---

## Answer Key
1. B
2. B
3. A

Brief explanation: Clue 5 gives order of Rio’s item, paperback book, jar, each followed by next in spot number sequence from 1 upward. Clue 4 puts scarf in spot farthest from entryway (spot 4), so first three spots hold Rio’s item, book, jar. Jar sits in spot 3 (never in end spot, matching clue 1).
