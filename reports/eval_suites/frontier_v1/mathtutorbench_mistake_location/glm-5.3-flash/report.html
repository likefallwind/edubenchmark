2

**Explanation:** The error occurs at Step 2. The equation "height of hill = 4 × depth of river" is incorrect. Since the depth (300m) is a quarter of the vertical distance between the riverbed and the peak, multiplying gives the *total* vertical distance from riverbed to peak: 4 × 300 = 1200m. However, the hill's base sits 300m above the seabed (riverbed), so the actual height of the hill is 1200 − 300 = **900m**, not 1200m. The correct relationship should be: height of hill = (4 × depth) − depth.
