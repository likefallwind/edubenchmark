基准介绍

MMTutorBench evaluates multimodal math tutoring: the model sees previous keyframe images, the current keyframe image, and a student's question, then must provide next-step guidance rather than a full solution. The response is structured into Insight Discovery, Operation Formulation, and Operation Execution.

Scoring uses the dataset's per-instance rubric with six binary criteria: insight_identification, operation_prescription, operation_execution, solution_scope_control, brevity, and coherence. A fixed LLM judge is selected with MMTUTORBENCH_JUDGE_MODEL (default MiniMax-M3); total score is 0-6.

主页：https://huggingface.co/datasets/Tangchiu/mmtutorbench

You are given previous images in chronological order, the current image, and the student's question. Identify the student's point of confusion and provide guidance on the single next key step. Do not reveal a lengthy full solution or multiple subsequent steps. Your response must use exactly these three sections: - [Insight Discovery]: extract the key detail in the student's current state and explain why it matters. - [Operation Formulation]: state the very next critical mathematical operation the student should perform. - [Operation Execution]: execute only that key operation and give the immediate result. Student's question: I've calculated the discriminant and it's -64. Since it's a negative number, I'm not sure what to do from here. How do I find the values for 'a' now?
参考答案[key detail]: The discriminant of the quadratic equation is `D = -64`. A negative discriminant indicates that the roots of the equation are not real numbers; they are a pair of complex conjugates. [key operation]: To find these roots, apply the quadratic formula, `a = (-b ± √D) / 2a`. [next step]: Substitute the known values into the formula: `a = (-(-4) ± √(-64)) / (2 * 1)`. Since `√(-64)` is equivalent to `√(64) * √(-1)`, which is `8i`, the expression becomes `a = (4 ± 8i) / 2`. This results in two roots for `a`: `a₁ = 2 + 4i` and `a₂ = 2 - 4i`.
