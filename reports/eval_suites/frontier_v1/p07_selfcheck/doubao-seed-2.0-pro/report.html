### Step 1: Define the total number of codes
- **Single-letter codes**: There are \( n \) unique single-letter codes (one for each letter).
- **Two-letter codes**: Since order does not matter and letters are distinct, this is the number of combinations of \( n \) letters taken 2 at a time, given by \( \binom{n}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2} \).
