2.  A pair of two different letters. Since the order does not matter and the letters must be different, this is a combination of 2 letters chosen from the $n$ available letters. The number of ways to do this is given by $\binom{n}{2} = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}$.

$$ \text{Total Codes} = n + \frac{n(n-1)}{2} $$
$$ \text{Total Codes} = \frac{2n + n^2 - n}{2} $$
$$ \text{Total Codes} = \frac{n^2 + n}{2} = \frac{n(n+1)}{2} $$
