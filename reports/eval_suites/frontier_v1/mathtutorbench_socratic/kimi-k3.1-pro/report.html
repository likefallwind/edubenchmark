参考答案How many eggs does Janet sell? ** Janet sells 16 - 3 - 4 = <<16-3-4=9>>9 duck eggs a day. How much does Janet make at the farmers' market? ** She makes 9 * 2 = $<<9*2=18>>18 every day at the farmer’s market. #### 18
