5. Ran a small MCQ prompt test.
   - Six items: 3 AGIEval + 3 MMLU.
   - Best setting is the simple prompt: 4/6 correct, 4/6 format-compliant, 1/6 empty.
   - Over-strict prompts or very small `max_tokens` made results worse.
   - Details: `reports/re_benchmark_v1/experiments/mcq_prompt_fix/README.md`.

## Important Interpretation Boundaries

- Current MiniMax full-pilot predictions were generated before the final MCQ prompt cleanup. They are useful as a smoke test, not a publishable leaderboard.
- Open teaching, grading, and safety tasks are judge-required; current reports only archive responses and qualitative samples.
- KT/protocol, code execution, and multimodal tasks should not be merged into text-only accuracy.
- Proxy datasets remain proxies: MMLU is not MMLU-Pro, EduEval Essay Scoring is not ASAP, and EduGuard is not a complete youth-safety benchmark.
